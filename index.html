A free-for-all platform fighter you play inside Hytale.

Up to four players pick a fighter, each with their own weapons and abilities, and try to knock each other off the map. Grab the Smash Crystal to unleash a final smash, and watch your footing, because the map crumbles from the edge in. Last fighter standing wins.
